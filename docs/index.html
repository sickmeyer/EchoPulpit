The pipeline

Six steps, zero standing infrastructure.

A scheduled check notices the livestream ended, a temporary server does the work, and then it's gone — nothing left running, nothing left to pay for, until the next sermon.

Stream ends

Your church's livestream wraps on YouTube, same as always.

Noticed within 15 min

A scheduled check spots the newly-ended video automatically.

A server wakes up

One temporary machine spins up just for this one video.

Transcribed & written

Captions or speech-to-text, then Claude writes the article.

Emailed to you

The PDF and Markdown land in your inbox, ready to publish.

Server disappears

It terminates itself. Nothing runs again until next Sunday.

What comes out the other end

Not a transcript. A finished article.

What the Wilderness Was Actually For

It's easy to read the wilderness years as punishment. But the text keeps circling back to a different word — provision. Forty years is a long time to keep being fed by hand…

“Man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD.” — Deuteronomy 8:3
Verified against KJV text

  • Captions first, Whisper as fallback

    Uses YouTube's own transcript when it's usable — only falls back to speech-to-text when it has to.

  • Every verse checked, not trusted

    Quoted scripture is verified against a KJV text after generation — misquotes get corrected, not published.

  • Calibrated to one preacher's voice

    A short style guide with real writing samples keeps the article sounding like your pastor, not a generic AI voice.

  • Ready to publish, not just a draft

    Markdown with SEO frontmatter and a formatted PDF land in your inbox — sensitive material is flagged for review, never silently softened.
